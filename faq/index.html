Pame FAQ

Can't find an answer here? See Support to contact us.

1. Where is my journey stored?

Only on your iPhone. Your places, country marks, visits, notes and “Lived here” periods are saved in the app's storage on your device, and the map and journal work offline. Pame has no server, so we don't receive your journey and can't see it.

2. Do I need an account?

No. Pame has no accounts. You can mark countries, save places and keep your journal without signing in to anything.

3. Is my journey backed up?

Pame doesn't make its own backups. If your iPhone is backed up with iCloud Backup or to a computer, that backup can include Pame's data. These backups are provided by Apple and managed by you in your iPhone's settings, not by us, and we can't access them.

4. Does deleting the app delete my journey?

Yes. Deleting the app removes your journey from that iPhone, and it can't be undone. We don't have a copy, so we can't recover it. If something isn't working, please contact us before deleting the app.

5. How do I remove things from my journey?

Open a place and choose Remove from map, or open one of its visits or “Lived here” periods to delete it. Country marks can be changed to Unmarked from the country's sheet.

6. Does Pame use my location?

Only when you tap Show my location. Pame then asks for permission (the first time) and uses your location once, to centre the map on where you are. Your location isn't saved or backed up, and it isn't sent to us. It never marks a country or adds a place. The coordinates of places you save come from the app's built-in place list. You can change location access at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

7. I'm moving to a new iPhone.

Please contact us before changing or erasing anything on your old iPhone, and we'll help where we can.

8. Where do the place names and maps come from?

Place names and locations are based on data from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), as shown in the app under Settings → About Pame → Acknowledgements & Licenses. Country shapes and boundary lines are based on Natural Earth (public domain). The map is built into the app; Pame doesn't download map tiles.
