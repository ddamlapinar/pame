Pame Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026
Last updated: 25 November 2026

Pame (available on the App Store as “Pame: Travel Map & Journal”) is developed by Didem Gülşen Damlapınar, an independent developer based in Türkiye (“we”, “us”, “our”).

This policy explains how Pame handles your information and the choices you have regarding your privacy. We are the data controller for the personal information we process as described in this policy.

The short version

  • Your journey stays yours. Your places, visits and notes are stored locally on your iPhone. There's no account to create, and we don't collect or have access to your journey data.
  • No ads, no tracking. Pame contains no advertising, third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools. We don't sell your personal information.
  • Location is optional. Pame uses your location only when you choose to see where you are on the map. The app doesn't store or transmit your location.
  • You're in control. You can manage or delete your journey data directly in the app. If you contact us for support, we use your information to respond to your request.

1. Information and how it's handled

Your journey

The places you save, country marks, visits, residence periods, notes and app preferences are stored locally on your iPhone.

The map and place information are included in the app. Pame does not download map tiles or place data from external services.

We do not receive or have access to your journey data and cannot retrieve, restore or delete it on your behalf.

Depending on your device settings, Pame's data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. These backups are managed by you and Apple, and we do not have access to them.

Location

When you tap “Show my location”, Pame may request permission to access your device's location while you're using the app.

Your location is used only to centre the map and display your current position. Pame does not store this information, transmit it to external services or automatically add it to your journey.

The coordinates of saved places come from Pame's built-in place list, not from your device's location.

You can change or revoke location permission at any time in your iPhone's Settings. Pame remains fully usable without location access.

Sharing and external links

When you share a journey card, you choose where and with whom to share it. We do not receive a copy.

If you open an external link from Pame, the destination website or service handles your information according to its own privacy policy.

Support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include.

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information or information unrelated to your support request.

Providing this information is voluntary. However, we may need certain details to respond to your request or investigate an issue.

Our website

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process your IP address and technical information provided by your browser to operate and secure its hosting service.

Our website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

For more information, see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

App Store information

Apple distributes Pame through the App Store and may provide us with aggregated usage, performance and crash reports through App Store Connect.

These reports depend on Apple's data collection practices and your device's privacy settings. We use them to understand and improve the app's performance.

For more information, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

2. How we use personal information

We use the limited personal information we receive for the following purposes:

  • Responding to support requests and resolving reported issues.
  • Maintaining and improving the reliability and performance of Pame.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

Where applicable, we process personal information based on our legitimate interests in providing support, maintaining the app and resolving issues, or where processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation.

We do not use personal information for advertising, profiling or marketing. We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

5. Your privacy choices and rights

You can manage your information in the following ways:

  • Journey data: Remove saved places, visits, residence periods and country marks directly in the app.
  • App data: Delete Pame from your iPhone to remove its locally stored data. Existing device backups may still contain a copy.
  • Location: Manage or revoke location permission in your device's Settings.
  • Support correspondence: Contact us to request access to or deletion of the personal information you have shared with us.

Depending on applicable data protection laws, you may also have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal information.
  • Request correction or deletion of your information.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Receive your information in a portable format, where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, contact us at support@pamejournal.com.

We cannot provide, modify or delete journey data on your behalf because we do not have access to it.

6. Children's privacy

Pame is a general-audience app and is not specifically directed at children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared personal information with us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to Pame, our privacy practices or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will always be available on this page, with the updated date shown above.

Where required by law, we will notify you of significant changes or obtain your consent before introducing new uses of your personal information.
