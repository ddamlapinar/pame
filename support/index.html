Pame Support

If you need help, have feedback or want to report a bug, please email us.

Contact us

Email: support@pamejournal.com

When you write, it helps to include:

  • the app version (Settings → About Pame → Version);
  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

If a screenshot helps explain the issue, feel free to include one. Hide any personal information you don't want to share. Never include passwords or verification codes.

We will never ask for your passwords or verification codes.

Frequently asked questions

Answers about where your journey is stored, backups, location and more are in the FAQ.

What happens to your message

When you contact us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to attach. We use them only to answer you and to fix problems you report. We reply by email and don't add you to any mailing list.

Support email is received and stored in our Google Workspace account, provided by Google. We delete routine support emails, their attachments and our replies no later than six months after your request is closed. Our Privacy Policy explains this, and the few cases where we keep a message longer.
